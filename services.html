Services

A simple guide to current services and starting prices. If you are unsure what to book, reach out and I can help choose the best fit for your nails.

Manicures

  1. Natural Manicure (no polish)

    Clean and simple manicure focussed on health of the natural nail. Includes shaping, cuticle care, buffing, and a relaxing hand massage.

    $40

  2. No-chip Manicure

    Long-lasting polish that won't chip, smudge, or dull. Includes nail shaping, cuticle care, and a relaxing hand massage.

    $50

  3. Gel-X

    Lightweight soft gel extension system that adds length and strength while maintaining a natural feel. Durable, glossy, and fully custom.

    $75

  4. Builder Gel

    Flexible strengthening gel applied over the natural nail to help promote growth and durability. Perfect for added structure without extensions.

    $65

  5. Hard Gel

    Durable enhancement designed for added strength, structure, and long-lasting wear. Ideal for stronger nails or longer lengths.

    $65

Pedicures

  1. Basic Pedicure

    Warm foot soak, cuticle care, toenail trimming and shaping, exfoliation, callus care, 5-minute massage, and gel polish application.

    $75

  2. Spa Pedicure

    Basic pedicure plus eco-wax treatment and an 8-minute massage including hot stones.

    $90
