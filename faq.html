FAQs

Have a question before your appointment? Here are a few helpful details about services, gel options, nail art, appointment prep, and custom press-ons so you can feel ready for your visit.

  1. Where are you located?

    Door County Nails is located at 2521 S Bay Shore Dr, Suite 8, Sister Bay, WI 54234.

  2. What nail services do you offer?

    I offer natural manicures, no-chip manicures, Gel-X extensions, builder gel, hard gel, basic pedicures, spa pedicures, nail art, enhancement removal, Eco-fin wax treatments, and custom reusable press-on nails. Please see the services page for more information and pricing.

  3. What is the difference between Gel-X, builder gel, and hard gel?

    Gel-X is a lightweight soft gel extension system that adds length with a natural feel. Builder gel is applied over your natural nails to add strength and structure without extensions. Hard gel is a durable enhancement option ideal for added strength, longer lengths, and long-lasting wear.

  4. Do you offer regular manicures without polish?

    Yes. The Natural Manicure is perfect if you want clean, healthy-looking nails without polish. It includes shaping, cuticle care, buffing, and a relaxing hand massage.

  5. How long does a no-chip manicure last?

    A no-chip manicure is designed to be long-lasting and typically lasts around 2 to 3 weeks, depending on your nail growth, lifestyle, and aftercare.

  6. Do you offer pedicures?

    Yes. I offer a Basic Pedicure, which includes a warm foot soak, cuticle care, toenail trimming and shaping, exfoliation, callus care, massage, and gel polish. I also offer a Spa Pedicure, which includes everything in the basic pedicure plus an Eco-fin wax treatment and an 8-minute massage with hot stones.

  7. Can I add nail art to my appointment?

    Yes. Nail art is available starting at $2 per nail. Pricing may vary depending on the detail, design, and time needed, so feel free to bring inspiration photos to your appointment.

  8. Do you remove old gel or nail enhancements?

    Yes. Enhancement removal is available for $20. Please let me know when booking if you currently have gel, Gel-X, builder gel, hard gel, or another product on your nails so enough time can be reserved.

  9. Do you make custom press-on nails?

    Yes. Custom reusable press-on nails are available starting at $30. They are a great option for special events, vacations, weddings, or anyone who wants a custom set without committing to salon enhancements.

  10. How should I prepare for my appointment?

    Please arrive with clean hands or feet and avoid trimming your cuticles beforehand. If you have a specific color, design, or nail shape in mind, bring photos for inspiration. If you have any nail concerns, product allergies, or past reactions, please mention them before your service begins.
