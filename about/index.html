About

Hi, I'm Darby Hibbard, a private nail technician based in Sister Bay, and the face behind Door County Nails.

I created this space to offer nail services that feel relaxed, personal, and safe. Whether you're a local looking for regular self-care, a bride celebrating a special weekend, or a visitor treating yourself while enjoying Door County, I'd love to have you book an appointment.

Door County has a special place in my heart, and I'm so grateful to build my business here and serve the people who make this area so special. When I'm not doing nails, you can usually find me spending time with my husband, planning our next trip, or playing a never-ending game of fetch with my dogs.

I enjoy the personal side of this work, and love helping people feel beautiful. To me, nails are a small but meaningful way to feel confident, put-together, and cared for. I enjoy helping each client find a look that fits their style, whether that's clean and classic, soft and natural, bold and colorful, or something special for a big event. Each appointment with me is fully tailored to you, your nail needs, and your design preferences.

I'm excited to welcome locals, seasonal residents, and Door County visitors. Whether you're preparing for your wedding day, celebrating a special occasion, or simply taking time for yourself, my goal is to make your appointment comfortable, enjoyable, and beautiful from start to finish.

I look forward to meeting you, and helping you feel your best, one service at a time.
